# ADR-0008: 3D Geometry Reality Check

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Deciders:** AI coding agent (M11.6)

## Context

The Great Pyramid and Osiris Shaft blockouts are authored as LOD0 data and regenerated by `database/geometry/gp-lod1.ts` for LOD1. During M11.6 verification (`scripts/verify-3d-geometry.ts`) several discrepancies appeared between the scene graph and the documented survey measurements:

1. **North-facing shafts pointed south.** `slopedBoxFromFloorEndpoints` used `Math.abs(dz)` when computing the X-rotation angle, so north-going shafts (`dz < 0`) were mirrored and their floor faces no longer sat on the intended start/end segment.
2. **Relieving chambers were stacked without masonry floor gaps.** The total KC-floor-to-Campbell-roof height was ~7.5 m too low compared with Vyse's 21.11 m total.
3. **Verification false positives.** The original `endpoints()` helper returned centreline endpoints instead of floor-face endpoints, causing the GG/AP junction and Osiris conduit checks to fail.
4. **One remaining modeling approximation.** The Queen's Chamber Passage is a single straight box (52.132 m) while Petrie records a 43.96 m gallery-to-chamber segment.

## Decision

1. Rewrite `slopedBoxFromFloorEndpoints` in `database/geometry/gp-geometry-utils.ts` to derive the unit direction in the Y-Z plane, preserve the sign of `dz`, and compute a Three.js X-rotation that keeps the box's local +Y upward and its floor face on the supplied endpoints.
2. Add `RELIEVING_MASONRY_GAP = 1.88` m to `database/geometry/gp-lod1.ts` between each relieving chamber so the stack top reaches the documented total height.
3. Update the LOD0 blockout in `database/blockouts/great-pyramid.ts` to match the corrected LOD1 output for north-shaft rotations and relieving-chamber positions.
4. Update `verify-3d-geometry.ts` to compute floor-face endpoints and to warn (not fail) on the QC passage length approximation.
5. Update `docs/architecture/great-pyramid-blockout-geometry.md` with the corrected positions, rotations, and shaft sizes.

## Consequences

- All four KC/QC north shafts now start at the correct chamber wall and slope north/up.
- Relieving chamber stack top is within 0.01 m of the documented Vyse total (64.08 m vs 64.07 m expected).
- `npx tsx scripts/verify-3d-geometry.ts` now reports **134 PASS, 1 WARN, 0 FAIL**.
- The QC passage length and any future multi-segment geometry remain documented warnings in the risk register.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Hardcode north-shaft positions/rotations only in the blockout.** Rejected because it would diverge from LOD1, breaking the LOD0/LOD1 consistency contract.
- **Leave relieving chambers without gaps and document it as a measurement uncertainty.** Rejected because the missing 7.5 m is a systematic structural offset, not a measurement tolerance.
- **Split the Queen's Chamber Passage into two boxes now.** Deferred; the current single-box is a known approximation and the user priority was shaft/reality verification.
