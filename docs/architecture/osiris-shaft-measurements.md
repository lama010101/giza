## Northern Conduit (NW corner of Chamber I)

Located in the **north-west corner of Chamber I**, this narrow passage is the only known continuation beyond the principal Osiris Shaft complex.

| Measurement | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Entrance location | NW corner of Chamber I | Cut at floor level |
| Entry cross-section | 0.5 m × 0.5 m to 0.6 m × 0.7 m | Approx. 20 in × 20 in |
| Initial width | Extremely narrow (crawl space) | Adult access impossible |
| Accessible length | 6.0–7.0 m | Explored before obstruction |
| Termination distance | ~6.8 m | Choked with mud, silt, and groundwater |
| Orientation | Northwesterly, toward Khafre Pyramid/Causeway axis | Follows natural bedrock fracture |
| Fill | Dense mud, washed-in silt, and groundwater | Prevents further exploration |
| Geological character | Appears to exploit a natural fissure enlarged by human work | Not a regularly quarried corridor |
| Excavation state | Incomplete | Tool marks cease before the blockage |
| Robotic investigation | Endoscopic and robotic probes attempted | No confirmed continuation beyond obstruction |
| Archaeological interpretation | Only known unfinished extension of the Osiris complex | Purpose remains unknown |

### Interpretation

Unlike every other chamber within the Osiris Shaft, which forms a geometrically self-contained complex, the Northern Conduit represents an abandoned attempt to continue excavation beyond Chamber I.

Current evidence suggests:

- excavation followed a natural fracture within the limestone;
- work ceased before completion;
- the blockage is composed primarily of compact mud and debris rather than finished masonry;
- no decorated walls, architectural elements, or artificial chambers have yet been identified beyond the accessible section.

For simulation purposes the conduit should therefore be classified as:

> **Partially explored natural fissure modified by human excavation.**

Confidence: **83**

Unknown continuation beyond obstruction: **Unknown (0 confidence).** Not evidence for hidden chambers.

## Notes

1. All measurements in meters unless otherwise noted (cm for sarcophagi and small features).
2. "c." (circa) indicates approximate values as published.
3. Wall surfaces are described as "roughly hewn" throughout — dimensions are maximum/clearance values, not precise.
4. The Hawass 2007 report is the primary archaeological source. The Whiting thesis cross-references Hawass and provides the Level 3 sarcophagus dimensions.
5. No laser scan or photogrammetric survey data is currently available. Future surveys may refine these measurements.
6. The causeway tunnel ("subway") dimensions are not fully documented in the Hawass report — only the position of Shaft A within it is given.
